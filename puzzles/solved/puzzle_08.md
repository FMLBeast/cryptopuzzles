## Sub-Puzzles & Solutions

### Puzzle 1: Grigori Rasputin
**Riddle**: [Description of someone who died in an extraordinary way involving multiple assassination attempts]

**Solution**: **Rasputin**

**Historical Facts**:
- Grigori Rasputin (1869-1916) was a Russian mystic and advisor to the Romanov family
- Famous for his extraordinarily difficult assassination
- According to accounts, assassins:
  - Fed him cyanide-laced cakes and wine (he survived)
  - Shot him multiple times (he kept getting up)
  - Clubbed him
  - Finally drowned him in the Neva River
- His death became legendary for how hard he was to kill

**Method**:
- Research historical figures with unusual death circumstances
- Cross-reference with famous assassination attempts
- Rasputin's death is one of the most famous in history

**Technique**: Historical research, famous death circumstances

### Puzzle 2: Kaiser Wilhelm II
**Riddle**: [Description related to historical figure's death or life circumstances]

**Solution**: **Wilhelm** (likely Kaiser Wilhelm II)

**Historical Facts**:
- Kaiser Wilhelm II (1859-1941) was the last German Emperor
- Abdicated in 1918 after WWI
- Lived in exile in the Netherlands until his death
- Died of natural causes (pulmonary embolism) in 1941 during WWII

**Possible Riddle Connections**:

### Puzzle 3: Alexander Alekhine
**Riddle**: [Description of death circumstances]

**Solution**: **Alekhine**

**Historical Facts**:
- Alexander Alekhine (1892-1946) was a Russian-French chess grandmaster
- Fourth World Chess Champion (1927-1935, 1937-1946)
- Died mysteriously in a hotel room in Estoril, Portugal in 1946
- Found dead in his hotel room, possibly choking on a piece of meat
- Death circumstances have been subject to speculation and mystery
- Some theories suggest assassination (he had controversial WWII associations)

**Method**:
- Research famous chess players
- Look for unusual or mysterious death circumstances
- Cross-reference with 20th century historical events

**Technique**: Chess history knowledge, biographical research, mysterious deaths
