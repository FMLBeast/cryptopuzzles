## Sub-Puzzles & Solutions

### 1. Wallet Problem (Algebra)
**Question**: Sam and Kyle are talking about their AR tokens. Sam says: "If I give you 1 AR, we'll have the same amount, but if you give me 1 AR, I'll have twice your amount."

Find: Kyle's and Sam's AR amounts

**Solution**: Kyle = **5** AR, Sam = **7** AR

**Method**:
- Let K = Kyle's amount, S = Sam's amount
- Equation 1: S - 1 = K + 1 → S = K + 2
- Equation 2: S + 1 = 2(K - 1) → S = 2K - 3
- Solving: K + 2 = 2K - 3 → K = 5, S = 7

### 2. Countdown Device (Pattern Recognition)
**Question**: A device starts counting down from 99:00. It beeps when the two groups of numbers are the same, or when the device shows a palindrome.

Find: What does the device show at the 99th beep?

**Solution**: **10:01**

**Method**:
- Device beeps when minutes = seconds (MM:SS where MM=SS)
- Also beeps on palindromes (e.g., 12:21, 13:31)
- Count backward from 99:00 tracking all valid beep conditions
- The 99th beep occurs at 10:01

### 3. Monk Ages (Algebra)
**Question**: Two monks are talking about their ages. The younger one says: "Take three times my age in three years and subtract three times my age three years ago." The older monk replies: "That's exactly my age!" The older monk's age equals the younger monk's age multiplied by five.

Find: The age of the youngest and oldest monk

**Solution**: Youngest = **18**, Oldest = **90**

**Method**:
- Let Y = younger monk's age
- Expression: 3(Y + 3) - 3(Y - 3) = 3Y + 9 - 3Y + 9 = 18
- Older monk's age = 18 × 5 = 90
