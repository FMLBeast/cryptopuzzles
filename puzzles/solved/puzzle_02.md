## Sub-Puzzles & Solutions

### 1. Square Counting Puzzle (Pattern Recognition)
**Description**: A visual puzzle involving counting squares formed by dots.

**Solution**: **11** squares

**Method**:
- Count all possible squares that can be formed from a grid of dots

- Total count: 11 squares

**Technique**: Visual pattern recognition, systematic enumeration

### 2. Mirroring/Reflection Puzzle (Time Reflection)
**Description**: A puzzle involving time displayed in a mirror or reflection.

**Question**: What time shows as 12:11 in a digital display?

**Solution**: **11:51**

**Method**:
- When a digital clock showing 12:11 is viewed in a mirror, the digits flip
- Digital segments mirror: 1→1, 2→5
- 12:11 mirrored becomes 11:51
- Understanding digital segment geometry is key

**Technique**: Mirror symmetry, digital display analysis

---

### 3. Chess FEN Notation Puzzle (Chess)
**Description**: A chess position given in FEN (Forsyth-Edwards Notation) format.

**Solution**: **Nfg3** (Knight from f-file to g3)

**Method**:
- FEN notation describes complete chess position
- Analyze the position to find the best or only legal move
- N = Knight, f = from f-file, g3 = destination square
- Understanding chess notation and position evaluation required

**Technique**: Chess FEN notation, tactical analysis

### 4. Cryptarithm Puzzle (Mathematical Logic)
**Description**: AR + PAPER = PIZZA (alphanumeric substitution)

Each letter represents a unique digit (0-9). Find the arithmetic equation.

**Solution**: **68 + 96938 = 97006**

**Method**:
- Assign digits to letters: A=6, R=8, P=9, E=3, I=7, Z=0
- AR = 68
- PAPER = 96938
- PIZZA = 97006
- Verification: 68 + 96938 = 97006 ✓

**Solving Strategy**:
1. Identify constraints (leading digits ≠ 0)
2. Analyze column additions for carries
3. Use logical deduction to narrow possibilities
4. Test combinations systematically
5. P must be 9 (5-digit + 2-digit = 5-digit)
6. Work through carries column by column

**Technique**: Cryptarithm solving, constraint satisfaction, logical deduction
